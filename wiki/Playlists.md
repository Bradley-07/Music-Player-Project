# Playlists (Web)

## Create a playlist
- Enter a name in the New playlist input.
- Click Create.

## Add songs
- Pick a target playlist in the Add to playlist dropdown.
- Click Add next to a song.

## Remove songs
- Click a playlist on the left to view it.
- Click Remove on any song to remove it from that playlist.

## Behavior details
- The special All Songs list always shows every loaded song.
- Playlists exist only in the current browser session.
- Shuffle selects a random next song and keeps a history for Previous.
